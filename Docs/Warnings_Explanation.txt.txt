 LINT-28 (unconnected port)
Warning: In design 'data_sampling_00000006_8_0000000b', port 'prescale[0]' is not connected to any nets. (LINT-28)

The LSB (prescale[0]) is unused in data_sampling.

i use prescale >> 1 instead of prescale/2 (to avoid multipliers)

That means bit[0] is always shifted out and never used.
Synthesis sees prescale[0] as redundant → reports it as LINT-28 unconnected port.
Not a problem as data sampling depends only on the middle bits of the prescale.
